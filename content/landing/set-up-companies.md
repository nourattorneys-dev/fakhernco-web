## Getting the structure right is the whole decision

Most company formation is sold as paperwork. It is not. The structure you choose
determines what you are allowed to trade, who can own what, how you are taxed, how
profits leave the country, and how easily you can exit later. Changing it afterwards
is expensive.

We advise on the structure first, then handle the formation.

## Which jurisdiction fits you

- **Mainland** — unrestricted access to the UAE market, the ability to trade directly
  with the local market and to bid for government work.
- **Free zone** — sector-focused hubs with their own regulators, including DIFC and
  ADGM for financial services, and options in Sharjah, RAK and across the Emirates.
- **Offshore** — for holding structures, asset protection and international trade
  rather than trading inside the UAE.

We will tell you honestly which one your business actually needs, including when the
cheapest option is the wrong one.

## What we do

- **Advise on jurisdiction, licensing and activity** so the licence matches what you
  will really be doing.
- **Draft the constitutional documents** — memorandum, articles, and the shareholder
  agreement that governs what happens when the founders disagree.
- **Handle registration end to end**, including approvals, notarisation and
  attestation.
- **Set up corporate governance** — board structure, signing authority, powers of
  attorney.
- **Advise on exit** — restructuring, share transfers, and the liquidation process,
  before you need them.

## How it works

1. **Tell us what the business will do**, where its customers are, and who owns it.
2. **Receive a written recommendation** on jurisdiction and structure, with costs.
3. **We form the company** and hand you a business that is properly documented.

## Why use a law firm rather than a formation agent

An agent files forms. A shareholder agreement that fails when two founders fall out,
a licence that does not cover the activity you are actually invoicing for, or a
structure that traps profits offshore are legal problems, and they surface later —
usually at the worst moment. We draft the documents that decide those outcomes.

## Common questions

**Can I own 100% of my company?**
In many activities and jurisdictions, yes. It depends on the activity and the
jurisdiction, which is exactly what we assess before recommending a structure.

**Do I need a shareholder agreement if it is just me and one partner?**
Especially then. Two-founder companies are the ones that most often end up in
dispute, and the agreement is what decides the outcome when they do.

**Can you also handle contracts and employment documents once we are set up?**
Yes — contracts, employment agreements and ongoing corporate matters are handled by
the same team.
