## Before you commit to anything

Most people come to us at one of two moments: just before signing something, or
just after something has gone wrong. The first is very much cheaper than the
second, and it is the one people skip.

A consultation is not a sales meeting. It is a working session in which a lawyer
reads your documents, tells you where you stand under UAE law, and sets out your
options — including the option of doing nothing, which is sometimes the right
advice.

## What a consultation covers

- Whether you have a claim, or a defence, and how strong it actually is
- What a contract you have been asked to sign commits you to
- Which deadlines apply, and how much time you genuinely have
- What the realistic outcomes are, rather than the best case
- What each route would cost, and whether it is economic to pursue
- What to do next, in order

## Who we advise

Businesses, investors and private individuals, local and international, across
litigation, company formation, contracts, corporate matters, real estate,
employment and criminal defence.

If your matter falls outside what we do, we will tell you that rather than take
it on.

## How it works

1. **Tell us the situation** and send anything relevant — contracts,
   correspondence, notices, court papers.
2. **We read it before we meet**, so the session is spent on advice rather than
   on you explaining background we could have read.
3. **You get a clear view** in plain language, and a written follow-up where the
   matter warrants one.

## What to bring

Whatever you have:

- Contracts and agreements, including drafts and earlier versions
- Any notice or letter you have received
- Correspondence with the other side, including WhatsApp messages
- Court or authority papers, if proceedings have started
- Company documents — licence, memorandum, shareholder agreement — for corporate
  matters

If you do not have the documents, come anyway. Part of the value of a first
meeting is being told precisely what to obtain and how.

## What we will tell you honestly

- When you do not need a lawyer
- When a claim is real but not economic to pursue
- When your position is weaker than you have been told elsewhere
- When the deadline has passed, and what if anything remains

A consultation that ends with "this is not worth pursuing" has still saved you
the cost of finding that out slowly.

## Confidentiality

Everything you share is confidential, whether or not you go on to instruct us.
Our non-conflict policy means we will tell you at the outset if we cannot act for
you, before you have told us anything you would rather we did not know.

## Common questions

**Am I obliged to instruct you afterwards?**
No. Many consultations end with us telling someone they do not need a lawyer.

**Can we meet remotely?**
Yes. In person in Abu Dhabi and Dubai, or by call or video where that is easier.

**Which languages?**
We advise in Arabic and English.

**How soon can I be seen?**
Tell us how urgent it is when you make contact. Matters running to a deadline are
prioritised.

**Can I bring a business partner or family member?**
Yes, but be aware that our duty of confidentiality is to our client. If two of
you may end up on opposite sides of the same matter, say so at the start and we
will tell you what that means.
