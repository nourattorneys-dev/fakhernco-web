## Before you commit to anything

Most people come to us at one of two moments: just before signing something, or just
after something has gone wrong. The first is much cheaper than the second.

A consultation is not a sales meeting. It is a working session in which a lawyer
reads your documents, tells you where you stand under UAE law, and sets out your
options — including the option of doing nothing.

## What a consultation covers

- Whether you have a claim, or a defence, and how strong it is
- What a contract you have been asked to sign actually commits you to
- Which deadlines apply, and how much time you have
- What the realistic outcomes are — not the best case
- What each route would cost, and whether it is economic
- What to do next, in order

## Who we advise

Businesses, investors and private individuals — local and international — across
litigation, company formation, contracts, corporate matters, real estate,
employment, and criminal defence.

## How it works

1. **Tell us the situation** and send anything relevant — contracts, correspondence,
   notices, court papers.
2. **We read it before we meet**, so the session is spent on advice rather than
   background.
3. **You receive a clear view**, in plain language, and a written follow-up where the
   matter warrants one.

## What you should bring

Whatever you have. Contracts and agreements, any notice or letter you have received,
correspondence with the other side, and court or authority papers if proceedings have
started. If you do not have documents, come anyway — we can tell you what to obtain.

## Confidentiality

Everything you share is confidential, whether or not you go on to instruct us. Our
non-conflict policy means we will tell you at the outset if we cannot act for you.

## Common questions

**Am I obliged to instruct you afterwards?**
No. Many consultations end with us telling someone they do not need a lawyer.

**Can we meet remotely?**
Yes. We meet in person in Abu Dhabi and Dubai, or by call or video where that is
easier.

**Which languages?**
We advise in Arabic and English.

**How soon can I be seen?**
Tell us how urgent it is when you make contact. Matters running to a deadline are
prioritised.
