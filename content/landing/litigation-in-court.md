## When a dispute has to go to court

Most disputes are better settled than litigated. But when the other side will not
negotiate, will not pay, or has already filed against you, you need a team that
knows how the UAE courts actually work — and that can be in the room.

You may be facing:

- A commercial or contractual dispute with a supplier, client or partner
- A debt that is owed and will not be paid
- A claim that has already been filed against you and is running to a deadline
- A judgment you need enforced, or one you need to appeal
- A dispute in a specialist forum — the DIFC courts, the Insurance Authority, the
  Central Bank

## The three tiers, and why the first one matters most

The UAE judicial system operates across three tiers, each with its own procedural
rules.

1. **Court of First Instance** — hears the case for the first time. This is where
   the evidence goes in, and it is the stage that decides most outcomes.
2. **Court of Appeal** — re-examines the case if either side is dissatisfied.
3. **Court of Cassation** — the highest court in most UAE jurisdictions. It does
   not re-hear the facts; it reviews whether the law was correctly interpreted
   and applied below.

The practical consequence is that the Court of First Instance is not a rehearsal.
Because Cassation reviews the law rather than the facts, evidence not properly
put before the first court is very difficult to introduce later. Cases are more
often lost there than won on appeal.

## Common questions

**How long does a case take?**
A straightforward commercial claim at the Court of First Instance typically takes
six to twelve months. An appeal adds to that, and Cassation adds more. We give
you a realistic timeline for your specific matter at the strategy stage rather
than an optimistic one.

**Do I have to attend court myself?**
Usually not. In most civil and commercial matters we appear on your behalf under
a power of attorney.

**What will it cost?**
You receive a written fee estimate before any work begins. We do not start work
and then present a bill.

**I have already been served. How urgent is this?**
Urgent. Court deadlines in the UAE are short and are not generally forgiving of a
party who was still looking for a lawyer. Bring the papers, including the
envelope or notification, as soon as you can.

**Can a foreign judgment be enforced here?**
Sometimes, depending on the country it came from and the treaties in place. It is
a specific question with a specific answer — bring the judgment and we will tell
you.
