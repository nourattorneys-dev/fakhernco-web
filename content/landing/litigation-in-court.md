## When a dispute has to go to court

Most disputes are better settled than litigated. But when the other side will not
negotiate, will not pay, or has already filed against you, you need a team that
knows how the UAE courts actually work — and that can be in the room.

You may be facing:

- A commercial or contractual dispute with a supplier, client or partner
- A debt that is owed and will not be paid
- A claim that has already been filed against you and is running to a deadline
- A judgment you need enforced, or one you need to appeal
- A dispute in a specialist forum — DIFC courts, the Insurance Authority, the Central Bank

## Common questions

**How long does a case take?**
A straightforward commercial claim at the Court of First Instance typically takes
six to twelve months. An appeal adds to that. We give you a realistic timeline for
your specific matter at the strategy stage, not an optimistic one.

**What are the three court tiers?**
The Court of First Instance hears the case for the first time. The Court of Appeal
re-examines it if either side is dissatisfied. The Court of Cassation is the highest
court and reviews whether the law was applied correctly, rather than re-hearing the
facts.

**Do I have to attend court myself?**
Usually not. In most civil and commercial matters we appear on your behalf under a
power of attorney.

**What will it cost?**
You receive a written fee estimate before any work begins. We do not start and then
present a bill.
