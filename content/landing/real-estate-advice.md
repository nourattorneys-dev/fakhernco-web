## The cheapest property dispute is the one you avoided

Almost every property dispute we litigate began with a document somebody signed
without advice — an off-plan agreement with no protection against delay, a lease with
an unenforceable renewal clause, a purchase where the title was never properly
checked.

This is advisory work, before the transaction, not litigation after it.

## What we advise on

- **Purchase and sale** — reviewing the sale agreement before you sign, and advising
  on what it actually obliges each side to do
- **Off-plan purchases** — payment schedules, handover obligations, and what happens
  if the developer is late
- **Title and due diligence** — ownership, encumbrances, mortgages and restrictions
- **Leases** — commercial and residential, for landlords and tenants, including
  renewal and exit terms
- **Developer and construction agreements**
- **Ownership structures** — including holding property through a company, and what
  that means for transfer and succession
- **Succession and inheritance** of UAE property, which does not work the way many
  owners assume

## What we do

- **Read the document you are about to sign** and tell you what it commits you to,
  in plain language.
- **Negotiate the terms that matter** — handover dates, delay compensation, exit
  rights, penalties.
- **Verify the property** and the seller's ability to actually transfer it.
- **Structure the ownership** so a future sale, transfer or inheritance is
  straightforward.
- **Draft bilingually**, in Arabic and English, because Arabic governs in a UAE
  court.

## How it works

1. **Send us the agreement** and tell us what the deal is meant to achieve.
2. **We review and advise**, flagging the clauses that carry real financial risk.
3. **We negotiate or redraft** where you want us to.

## Common questions

**The developer says the contract is standard and cannot be changed. Is that true?**
Sometimes it is, in which case the value of advice is knowing exactly what you are
accepting. Often it is not, and terms are negotiable — particularly on delay and
payment.

**Should a foreign buyer own property personally or through a company?**
It depends on the emirate, the area, your tax position and your succession plans.
It is worth deciding before you buy rather than restructuring afterwards.

**What happens to my UAE property if I die?**
Not necessarily what you expect. This is one of the most common and most costly
assumptions property owners make here, and it is worth an hour of advice.

**Can you also act if it does become a dispute?**
Yes — the same team handles real estate and rental disputes.
