## Property disputes run to deadlines

Rental and property claims in the UAE are governed by strict procedures and short
windows — for notices, for filing, and for appeals. Missing one can decide the
matter regardless of who was right. That is the single most common reason a good
case is lost.

## Disputes we handle

- **Landlord and tenant** — rent increases, eviction notices, renewal disputes,
  security deposits, unpaid rent
- **Sale and purchase** — breach of the sale agreement, failure to complete,
  deposit disputes
- **Off-plan and developer claims** — delayed handover, failure to deliver, changes
  to the unit or the project
- **Snagging and construction defects** in a delivered property
- **Service charges** and owners' association disputes
- **Joint ownership and inheritance** of property

## What we do

- **Check the deadlines first.** Before anything else, we establish what notice was
  served, when, and what window you are in.
- **Serve or challenge notices correctly**, because a defective notice can end a
  claim on its own.
- **File before the competent rental dispute authority** or the relevant court.
- **Handle developer and off-plan claims**, including compensation for delay.
- **Enforce the outcome**, including eviction and recovery of sums awarded.

## How it works

1. **A confidential consultation.** Bring the tenancy contract or sale agreement,
   the title deed if you have it, and any notice you have received or sent.
2. **A written assessment** — the merits, the deadline you are working to, the
   realistic outcome and the cost.
3. **We act**, and keep you updated at every stage.

## Common questions

**I have received an eviction notice. How long do I have?**
That depends on the ground for eviction and the notice served. Bring the notice to
the consultation — the date on it matters more than almost anything else in the file.

**My developer has missed the handover date. Do I have a claim?**
Often, yes, and it may include compensation. It depends on the terms of the sale
agreement and the length and cause of the delay.

**Can my landlord increase the rent by any amount?**
No. Increases are regulated and must follow the applicable rules and notice periods.
We will tell you whether the increase you have been given is lawful.

**Is it worth going to a hearing over a deposit?**
Sometimes it is not, and we will say so. We would rather tell you a claim is not
economic than run it for you.
