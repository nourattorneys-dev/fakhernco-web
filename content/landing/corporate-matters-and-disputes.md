## When the dispute is inside the company

A shareholder dispute is not an ordinary commercial disagreement. The people on the
other side have access to the accounts, the staff, the bank mandate and the
customers. Every week it continues, the business is worth less.

We act quickly and confidentially in matters including:

- Shareholder and partner disputes, including deadlock
- Removal or resignation of directors and managers
- Disputes over profit distribution, valuation or share transfers
- Breach of a shareholder or joint venture agreement
- Minority shareholder rights and oppression
- Corporate restructuring and exit

## What we do

- **Establish your position first.** What the constitutional documents and the
  shareholder agreement actually entitle you to, before anyone escalates.
- **Protect the company's assets** while the dispute runs — bank mandates, signing
  authority, access to records.
- **Negotiate a structured exit** where one is possible, because a bought-out
  shareholder is almost always cheaper than a litigated one.
- **Litigate when it is necessary**, before the UAE courts or the relevant
  specialist forum.
- **Restructure the governance** that allowed the dispute to happen, so it does not
  recur with the next partner.

## How it works

1. **A confidential consultation.** Bring the shareholder agreement, the licence and

2. **A written strategy** with the realistic outcomes and what each one costs.

## Common questions

**My partner has locked me out of the company accounts. What can I do?**
There are urgent steps available, and they work better the sooner they are taken.
This is a same-week matter, not a next-month one.

**We have no shareholder agreement. Is there anything I can do?**
Yes. Your rights then come from the constitutional documents and UAE company law
rather than from a private agreement. It is a harder position, not a hopeless one.

**Can this be resolved without going to court?**
Often, and usually that is the better outcome. A negotiated exit protects the
business's value; litigation between owners rarely does.
